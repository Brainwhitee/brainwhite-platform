# Page Map: Noth.in Reference

## 01. PRELOADER
- **CONTENT**: Minimal brand mark, loading percentage.
- **VISUAL**: Stark monochrome.
- **INTERACTION**: Percentage counts up, logo masks in.
- **TRANSITION**: Wipes up/fades out to reveal the Hero instantly.
- **NEXT SECTION**: Hero

## 02. HERO
- **CONTENT**: Massive typographic statement, small editorial labels.
- **VISUAL**: Full viewport height. High contrast. Negative space.
- **INTERACTION**: Staggered text entrance. Custom cursor active.
- **TRANSITION**: Scrolls up normally, but text might slightly parallax down.
- **NEXT SECTION**: Manifesto

## 03. MANIFESTO
- **CONTENT**: High-impact brand philosophy.
- **VISUAL**: Huge typography filling the screen.
- **INTERACTION**: Scrub-based text reveal (words light up as you scroll).
- **TRANSITION**: Container pins to the screen until text is fully revealed, then unpins.
- **NEXT SECTION**: Work/Product Reveal

## 04. WORK / PRODUCT REVEAL (HORIZONTAL)
- **CONTENT**: Showcasing key capabilities or case studies.
- **VISUAL**: Large edge-to-edge imagery.
- **INTERACTION**: Vertical scroll translates into horizontal movement.
- **TRANSITION**: Once horizontal scroll completes, vertical scrolling resumes.
- **NEXT SECTION**: Details / Features

## 05. FEATURE STORYTELLING
- **CONTENT**: Specific details about the offering.
- **VISUAL**: Asymmetric grid. Text on one side, floating/parallax images on the other.
- **INTERACTION**: Images scale up slightly on hover. Text lines reveal on scroll intersection.
- **TRANSITION**: Smooth standard scroll with parallax overlaps.
- **NEXT SECTION**: Social Proof

## 06. SOCIAL PROOF / METRICS
- **CONTENT**: Numbers, client list.
- **VISUAL**: Minimalistic table or massive numbers.
- **INTERACTION**: Numbers tick up or reveal on scroll. Marquee effect for client names.
- **TRANSITION**: Normal scroll into footer.
- **NEXT SECTION**: CTA / Footer

## 07. CTA / FOOTER
- **CONTENT**: Final call to action, navigation links, social.
- **VISUAL**: Oversized CTA button/text.
- **INTERACTION**: Footer reveals from *underneath* the previous section (curtain effect). Magnetic hover on the massive CTA.
- **TRANSITION**: End of page.
